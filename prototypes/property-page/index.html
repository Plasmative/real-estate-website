Overview

One hundred hectares of rolling hills above the Pacific, six kilometres from the beach. Three natural plateaus look west over the coastline, each wide enough for a main residence or a small group of villas, with the sunset framed between two ridges.

Around 40% of the land is protected dry tropical forest, home to howler monkeys, white-tailed deer and motmots. The rest is open pasture, crossed by four kilometres of internal roads. Two natural springs and a seasonal river with about 1.2 km of frontage run through the lower valley.

The property has a single registered title, electricity at the entrance and a public access road. It suits a private estate, a boutique eco-resort or a long-term reforestation project, subject to the usual permits.
